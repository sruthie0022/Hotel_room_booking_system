SINCE 1928PORTLAND, OREGON

Rooted here.
Open to everywhere.

Aurelia House began as a family home on a quiet Portland lane. Nearly a century later, its spirit remains: generous rooms, thoughtful hospitality, and a belief that the best journeys leave space for the unexpected.

We work with neighborhood makers, cook with the seasons, and take care of the little things so you can make the most of the big ones. Whether you’re here for one night or a long weekend, there’s a place at the table for you.
